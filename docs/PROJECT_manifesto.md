# The "Why" and "What"

## Vision
To provide a fast, local-first time tracking solution for individuals who need simple project-based time logging without internet dependencies or complex overhead.

## Problem Solved
Traditional time trackers are often bloated or require constant connectivity. This tool provides a beautiful, responsive, and immediate local experience for tracking focused work.

## Roadmap
1. Core Timer & Project Management
2. Dashboard & Reporting
3. Data Export/Import
